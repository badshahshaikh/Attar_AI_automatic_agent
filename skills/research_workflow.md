# Research Workflow — MS Fragrances

## Source priority
1. Official MS Fragrances sources (YouTube, Instagram, website).
2. Direct business documentation.
3. Verified product/inventory records (Google Sheet).
4. Official manufacturer websites.
5. Authoritative fragrance industry sources.
6. Reputable educational sources.
7. Other secondary sources.

## Research process
1. Collect source (URL, platform, author, date).
2. Extract evidence (transcripts, captions, descriptions, timestamps).
3. Classify claim (business identity, product, customer experience, etc.).
4. Assign verification status: VERIFIED / PARTIALLY_VERIFIED / UNVERIFIED / CONFLICTING / OUTDATED.
5. Record in evidence_registry.json with source_id reference.
6. Register source in source_registry.json.
7. Build customer question bank entries with answer types.

## Rules
- Never manufacture research findings.
- Every factual claim must have traceable evidence.
- Distinguish source authenticity, claim verification, current validity, business approval.
- For YouTube: collect transcripts, preserve timestamps, distinguish spoken vs summary.
- For Instagram: only publicly accessible content; record post URLs.
- If a source cannot be accessed, report limitation; do not fabricate.
- External brand info (Ajmal, Surrati) = general education only, never attributed to MS Fragrances.
- Unverified statements must not become verified business facts.
